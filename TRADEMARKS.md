# snugcode trademark policy

The code in this repo is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name and brand** are not part of that license.
This policy protects users: when someone sees "snugcode", they should be able to trust that it is the real project.

"snugcode", the snugcode logo and icons, and the snugcode.dev domain are trademarks of the snugcode project
(arfakaisar).

## You can, without asking

- Say that your project is "based on snugcode", "a fork of snugcode", or "compatible with snugcode".
- Use the name in articles, tutorials, videos, reviews, and talks about snugcode.
- Run unmodified snugcode for yourself or your team and call it snugcode.
- Link to snugcode.dev or this repo.

## You can't, without written permission

- Publish a modified version, fork, or hosted service under the name "snugcode" or a confusingly similar name
  (for example "snugcode Pro", "snug-code", or "snugcoder").
- Use the snugcode logo or icons for your fork, product, or service. Replace the files in `web/icon*` and `docs/images/`
  with your own.
- Imply that your product or service is official, endorsed, or run by snugcode.
- Use the name in a domain, app store listing, package name, or social account in a way that suggests it is official.

## Forks

Forks are welcome. Before you publish one:

1. Pick a new name and logo, and replace them in the PWA (`web/`), the CLI output (`daemon/`), and the docs.
2. Point the defaults (`daemon/defaults.js`) at your own relay. The hosted relay at snugcode.dev is for official
   snugcode clients only.
3. Keep the AGPL-3.0 license and the copyright notices, as the license requires.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
